## Design Context

### Users
A remote solution architect needs a personal native macOS planner for project delivery, presales, and product work. The supplied PRD defines daily capture, explicit prioritization, feasible schedules, and personal routines.

### Brand Personality
Warm, focused, mythical. The user's name is The One Ring Planner, with a gold ring inspired by LOTR and playful routine feedback.

### Aesthetic Direction
A photoreal polished gold ring is the visual signature. Warm charcoal and parchment surfaces follow the system appearance, paired with Palatino display typography and Avenir Next body text. Native controls, readable timelines, sparse dividers, generous spacing, and a persistent macOS menu bar entry. The product remains a practical planner, with a little fantasy in the details.

### Design Principles
1. A single timeline is the main workspace; secondary information stays quiet.
2. Show real local persistence and integration state precisely.
3. Capture stays fast; classification and planning require explicit choices.
4. Gold highlights decisions and the ring; routine celebrations are brief and respect Reduce Motion.
5. Support keyboard capture, VoiceOver labels, light and dark appearances.

### Composition and interaction
Visual thesis: a quiet personal study, with warm materials and one engraved gold ring.
Content plan: sidebar navigation, a central daily timeline, and a persistent Inbox rail on the right. Daily capacity is summarized above the timeline, with detail available on demand.
Interaction thesis: a gentle ring entrance, crisp selection transitions, and instrument or movement flourishes when a routine is completed.

### UI revision
Inbox is a persistent right sidebar, including capture and per-task assignment menus. Every matrix task has a priority dropdown. Drag-and-drop highlights a target quadrant; incomplete records open a review sheet and remain in place until confirmed. Use photoreal gold ring artwork with a smooth rounded band and physical depth in place of the original drawn hoop.
